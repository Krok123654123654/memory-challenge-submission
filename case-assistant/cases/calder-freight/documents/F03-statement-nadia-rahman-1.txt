I have worked in accounts payable at Calder Freight since 2021. Between February and September 2025 I processed 23 invoices from Ortega Facility Services Ltd. Clara Voss approved every one of them.

Clara told me to keep each invoice under £10,000 and to split larger ones by depot. I thought this was normal practice at the time.

I first became worried on 15 September 2025, when the same work order number appeared on two invoices. I told Clara about it that day.
