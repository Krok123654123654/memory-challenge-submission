During the interim audit we identified 23 invoices from Ortega Facility Services Ltd, dated between 20 February 2025 and 12 September 2025, totalling £198,400. Every invoice was below £10,000 and approved by the Finance Director alone.

For 17 of the 23 invoices we found no site log, purchase order or delivery record showing that the work was done.

We recommend an investigation.
