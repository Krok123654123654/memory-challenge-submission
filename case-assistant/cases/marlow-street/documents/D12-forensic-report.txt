The rear door bears tool marks consistent with a flat bar about 20 mm wide.

A partial glove print was recovered from the door frame. It is not suitable for identification.

Grey cotton fibres were recovered from the splintered frame. They are consistent with a hooded top but could come from any similar grey cotton garment.

Tyre impressions in the lane are consistent with a light commercial van. They cannot be attributed to a specific vehicle.

No fingerprints were identified.
