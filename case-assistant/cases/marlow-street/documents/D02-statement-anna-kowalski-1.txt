I live at 12 Marlow Lane, directly behind the gallery store. On Saturday 14 March I took my dog out into the back lane at around 22:15.

I saw a white van with a ladder rack parked by the rear door of the gallery store. A man in a grey hooded top was carrying something flat and wrapped in a blanket to the back of the van.

I did not see his face. He was average height and wore gloves. I did not see a second person.

My husband Tomasz was asleep upstairs at the time and did not see anything.
