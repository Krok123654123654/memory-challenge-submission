title: Witness statement of Tomasz Kowalski
kind: statement
date: 2026-03-16
author: tomasz-kowalski
---
I live at 12 Marlow Lane with my wife Anna Kowalski. On the night of 14 March I went to bed at about 22:30.

I did not see any van myself. My wife told me the next morning that she had seen a white van and a man loading it behind the gallery.

At about 23:10 I was woken briefly by an engine starting in the lane. I did not look out of the window.
