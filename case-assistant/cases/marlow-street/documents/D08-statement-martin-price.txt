kind: statement
date: 2026-03-19
author: martin-price

I am a courier for Swift Fine Art Transport. On 14 March I delivered a wooden crate to the Marlow Street Gallery store at 16:30. Helen Marsh signed for it.

When I arrived, a white van was parked in the lane with builders' materials in the back. I took it to belong to someone doing work on the houses opposite. It had gone by the time I left at 16:50.

I did not notice the registration or any damage to that van.
