Interview commenced 14:15 at Marlow Road police station. Present: DS Brennan, Daniel Reyes, duty solicitor.

DS Brennan: Your phone connected to a mast near Marlow Street between 22:48 and 23:22 on 14 March. Last week you said you were at home all evening. Can you explain that?
Daniel Reyes: Fine, I did go out. I was selling my motorbike and arranged to meet a buyer near the canal. The buyer never turned up.
DS Brennan: How did you get there?
Daniel Reyes: Sam drove me in his van. We waited about half an hour and came home.
DS Brennan: Why did you say last week that you didn't go out?
Daniel Reyes: I didn't want to drag my brother into this.

Interview concluded 14:58.
