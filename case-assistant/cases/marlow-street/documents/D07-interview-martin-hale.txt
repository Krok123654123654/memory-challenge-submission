Interview commenced 09:30 at the gallery store office. Present: DS Brennan, Martin Hale.

DS Brennan: Talk me through your shift on 14 March.
Martin Hale: I started at 20:00. I did a full round at 22:00 and everything was locked. At about 22:40 I got a call that my mother had been taken to hospital, so I left the site.
DS Brennan: When did you come back?
Martin Hale: About 23:30. I didn't do another round until the morning because the panel only showed a fault, not a break-in.
DS Brennan: What did the panel show?
Martin Hale: A fault from 23:05. I thought it was the power again; it happens a few times a year.
DS Brennan: Did you tell Ms Marsh you had left the site?
Martin Hale: Not that night. I told her on the 15th.

Interview concluded 10:05.
