Interview commenced 10:02 at Marlow Road police station. Present: DS Brennan, Daniel Reyes, duty solicitor.

DS Brennan: Where were you on the evening of Saturday 14 March?
Daniel Reyes: At home all evening. I watched the football with my brother Sam. I didn't go out.
DS Brennan: Do you own or have access to a van?
Daniel Reyes: I don't own a van. My brother has a van for his building work, but I don't drive it.
DS Brennan: Have you ever been to the Marlow Street Gallery store?
Daniel Reyes: No. I know where it is, that's all.

Interview concluded 10:41.
