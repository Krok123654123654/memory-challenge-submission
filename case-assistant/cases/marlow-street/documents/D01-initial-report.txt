At 06:40 on 15 March 2026 I responded to a call from Martin Hale, night security guard at the Marlow Street Gallery store, 4 Marlow Lane.

The rear door of the store had been forced with a flat tool. The lock housing was bent and the door frame splintered at waist height.

Martin Hale told me the alarm panel showed a fault from 23:05 the previous night and that he had assumed it was a power glitch. He found the door open at 06:30 on his morning round.

The owner, Helen Marsh, attended at 07:20 and stated that three paintings and a bronze sculpture were missing from the storage racks.
